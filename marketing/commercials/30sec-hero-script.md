# SABACOS — 30-Second Hero Commercial
## "Your Glow, Delivered" (Apple/Nike-style viral spot)

**Format:** 30 seconds, 9:16 vertical (TikTok / Reels / Shorts / Telegram). 16:9 cutdown notes at the end.
**Tone:** Minimal. Rhythmic. Confident. No shouting, no market-noise clichés.
**Language:** English VO with Amharic on-screen lines + Amharic VO alt provided.
**Music:** 95–100 BPM minimal Afro-urban beat (krar sample + soft kick). Licensed or royalty-free.

---

## 1. Logline

A busy Addis Ababa university student orders 100% original glow products inside Telegram in seconds, pays only half upfront, and gets them at her door — while the city moves around her.

## 2. Character

| | |
|---|---|
| **Name / age** | Selam, 21 |
| **Who she is** | AAU business student, dorm life, part-time hustler, camera-confident |
| **Look** | Natural hair or clean puffs, minimal makeup at start → fresh-faced glow at end; white tee + jeans + denim jacket (wardrobe stays same all day = one shoot day) |
| **Energy** | Calm, in-control, slightly playful. Never salesy. She doesn't explain — she *does*. |

Supporting (no lines): delivery courier (bajaj driver with Sabacos parcel), roommate (1 reaction shot).

## 3. Locations (all in Addis, one day)

1. **Dorm room, morning** — bed, small mirror/shelf, window light.
2. **Campus walkway** — trees, students passing (movement).
3. **Bajaj street handoff** — any recognizable Addis street; courier arrives.
4. **Dorm doorstep, golden hour** — parcel handover + final glow shot.

## 4. Scene-by-scene script (30.0s)

Total VO: ~45 words. Every cut lands on the beat.

### SCENE 1 — THE HOOK (0.0–0:03)
- **Visual:** Extreme close-up — Selam wipes a cheap, irritated-looking foundation swatch off her cheek. Hard cut to black. One frame of white text: **"Stop."**
- **Camera:** Macro lens/phone macro, handheld micro-shake. Snap cut.
- **Sound:** Music BEGINS with a single krar pluck + breath. No VO.
- **On-screen:** `Stop.` (then cut)
- **Purpose:** Pattern interrupt. Retention hook in first second.

### SCENE 2 — THE PROBLEM (0:03–0:07)
- **Visual:** Quick 3-cut montage: (a) dusty Merkato-style stall with unlabelled creams, (b) Selam squinting at a smudged label, (c) phone screen: "100% original?" search.
- **Camera:** Fast whip-pans, 0.5s each. Slight desaturation on stall shots (grade only).
- **VO (calm, EN):** *"Fake makeup eats your skin."*
- **VO (AM alt):** *«ሐሰተኛ መዋቢያ ቆዳሽን ይበላል።»*
- **On-screen:** `FAKE?` stamped over the stall shot.

### SCENE 3 — THE RITUAL (0:07–0:14) — *hero of the spot*
- **Visual:** Selam on her bed, morning light. Phone screen-record style overlay: she taps the Sabacos bot → Shop opens → serum in cart → Chapa pay screen → **"Pay half now"** toggle ON. Real screen capture, 3 taps, each tap lands on a beat.
- **Camera:** Over-shoulder, shallow focus; phone UI­r magnified in post (no fake mockups — real app).
- **SFX:** Three soft tap clicks, cash-register *ching* pitched down on the half-pay toggle.
- **VO (EN):** *"Sabacos. Originals only. Half now, half at your door."*
- **VO (AM alt):** *«ሳባኮስ። ኦርጅናል ብቻ። ግማሽ አሁን፣ ግማሽ በደጃፍሽ።»*
- **On-screen (burned into the screen-record):** `50% NOW` badge pulses once.

### SCENE 4 — THE CITY MOVES (0:14–0:20)
- **Visual:** Montage on the beat: Selam walking campus in slow-mo (hair moves, city alive) → match-cut to bajaj wheel spinning → courier with Sabacos parcel → Addis skyline whip.
- **Camera:** Gimbal follow at 60fps conformed to 24; match cuts on circular motion (wheel → parcel tape → sun).
- **VO (EN):** *"Ordered in class. Delivered in Addis."*
- **VO (AM alt):** *«በክፍል ውስጥ ታዝዟል። በአዲስ አበባ ይደርሳል።»*
- **On-screen:** `1–3 DAYS` lower third, small.

### SCENE 5 — THE GLOW (0:20–0:26)
- **Visual:** Golden-hour doorstep. Courier hands parcel. Selam opens it, holds serum to camera, smiles — skin glowing. Roommate peeks, impressed (comedy beat, 0.5s).
- **Camera:** Backlit wide → push-in to product hero shot. Lens flare allowed.
- **Music:** Beat drops to half-time, warm pad under.
- **VO (EN):** *"Your glow, delivered."*
- **VO (AM alt):** *«ውበትሽ፣ በደጃፍሽ።»*
- **On-screen:** `YOUR GLOW, DELIVERED.`

### SCENE 6 — THE CTA (0:26–0:30)
- **Visual:** Black screen. Sabacos wordmark fades in. Below it, letter by letter: **t.me/sabacosbot**. Telegram paper-plane whoosh.
- **Camera:** Static. Let the brand breathe.
- **VO (EN):** *"Sabacos — shop inside Telegram."*
- **VO (AM alt):** *«ሳባኮስ — በቴሌግራም ውስጥ ግዛ።»*
- **On-screen:** `SABACOS` / `t.me/sabacosbot` / `100% ORIGINAL • PAY HALF NOW`

**End card hold 1.5s for the link to be screenshotted.**

## 6. Needed resources

**Cast (2):** Selam-type lead (student look, comfortable on camera); courier (real bajaj driver works — authentic); roommate (friend, 1 shot).
**Wardrobe:** White tee, jeans, denim jacket, clean sneakers (continuity all day). No logos.
**Props:** 2–3 real Sabacos products (serum hero + box), branded parcel/bag + tape, iPhone/Android with Telegram + Sabacos bot logged in, dorm shelf + mirror.
**Locations:** 1 dorm room (morning light), campus walkway (permit if needed), 1 street corner, doorstep at golden hour. All free/guerrilla-friendly.
**Crew (minimum viable):** Director/DP (one person), sound (phone + lav mic for VO — record VO separately in a quiet room), PA/runner.
**Gear:** Mirrorless or flagship phone + gimbal, 24/35/50mm or equivalents, 1 LED panel + reflector (dorm fill), lav mic.
**Post:** Edit (beat-cut), grade (desat stall vs warm glow), screen-record composites, licensed 95–100 BPM track, Amharic + English caption files (.srt both).
**Budget steer (Addis, 2026):** Guerrilla (friends + phone + gimbal): ~5,000–15,000 ETB. Semi-pro (DP + sound + grade): ~40,000–90,000 ETB.

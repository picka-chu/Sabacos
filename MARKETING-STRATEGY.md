# Sabacos Marketing Strategy — Launch Playbook for Addis Ababa

> **Target:** Launch Sabacos as the go-to cosmetics e-commerce platform in Addis Ababa.
> **Core audience:** Urban youth 18-35, fashion-conscious, Telegram-first, Amharic-speaking.
> **Launch date:** TBD — this playbook covers pre-launch (4 weeks), launch week, and post-launch (4 weeks).

---

## 1. Ethiopian Social Media Landscape (2026)

### Platform breakdown

| Platform | Users (Ethiopia) | Primary use | Why it matters for Sabacos |
|----------|-----------------|-------------|---------------------------|
| **TikTok** | 10M+ | Entertainment, comedy, trends | Dominant discovery engine for youth. Viral product reviews drive impulse buys. |
| **Instagram** | 5M+ | Lifestyle, beauty, fashion | Visual catalog. Stories = flash sales. Reels = tutorials. |
| **Telegram** | 15M+ | Commerce, communities, bots | Where Sabacos lives. Channel = broadcast list. Groups = community. |
| **Facebook** | 8M+ | News, groups, marketplace | Older demographic (25-40). Marketplace for local discovery. |
| **YouTube** | 6M+ | Long-form reviews, tutorials | SEO-driven. Makeup tutorials, product comparisons. |
| **Twitter/X** | 2M+ | Trending topics, hot takes | Real-time engagement, meme culture. |

### Consumer behavior

- **Mobile-first:** 90%+ of internet access is via mobile. All content must be vertical/mobile-optimized.
- **Cash-on-delivery dominant:** Most buyers prefer COD. Telegram invoices (Chapa) bridge the gap.
- **Social proof is everything:** Ethiopian buyers trust peer recommendations, not ads. UGC > branded content.
- **Amharic-first:** English captions perform well for branding, but Amharic captions drive engagement and shares.
- **Price sensitivity:** Mid-range pricing wins. Free delivery threshold is a strong motivator.
- **Telegram commerce norm:** Many businesses in Addis sell via Telegram channels + manual order collection. Sabacos automates this — a strong selling point.

### Viral content formats that work in Ethiopia

1. **"Before & After" transformations** — makeup, skincare, hair care
2. **Comedy skits with product placement** — Ethiopian comedy is huge; product integration feels natural
3. **"Get Ready With Me" (GRWM) in Amharic** — personal, relatable, shareable
4. **Price reveal/drop videos** — "I found this for X birr" triggers DMs
5. **Customer unboxing reactions** — authentic, user-generated
6. **Trend-jacking** — hopping on Ethiopian/African trending sounds and formats
7. **Mukbang-style product reviews** — casual, conversational, addictive
8. **Cultural moments** — Irreecha, Timkat, Enkutatash, Fasika tie-ins

## 2. Pre-Launch Phase (4 Weeks Before Launch)

### Goal: Build anticipation, collect waitlist, establish brand presence

#### Week 1 — Brand Teaser

**Social posts (3-5 pieces):**

- **Post 1 — Mystery teaser (TikTok + Instagram Reels)**
  - Visual: Close-up of lips applying lipstick, slow motion, no face reveal
  - Caption (Amharic): "መቼ መቼ ይመጣል? 🤔 አዲስ ነገር ይመጣል…" / (English: "When when? Something new is coming…")
  - Audio: Trending Ethiopian sound
  - CTA: "Follow for the reveal"

- **Post 2 — Brand name reveal (All platforms)**
  - Visual: Animated logo reveal — pink/neon aesthetic
  - Caption: "SABACOS ✨ Your beauty, your way. Coming soon to Telegram."
  - Hashtags: #Sabacos #AddisBeauty #CosmeticsAddis

- **Post 3 — Problem statement (Instagram Carousel / TikTok)**
  - Slide 1: "Tired of guessing if cosmetics are real?" (Amharic)
  - Slide 2: "Tired of waiting weeks for delivery?"
  - Slide 3: "Tired of sketchy payments?"
  - Slide 4: "Sabacos solves all of it. Telegram bot. Instant delivery. Verified products."
  - CTA: "Link in bio — join the waitlist"

- **Post 4 — Founder story (IGTV / YouTube Short)**
  - 60-second video: Why we built Sabacos
  - Authentic, casual, Amharic with English subtitles
  - Humanizes the brand

- **Post 5 — Telegram channel launch**
  - Create `@SabacosShop` (or similar)
  - Pin message: "Welcome to Sabacos! We're launching soon. Drop a ✨ if you're ready."
  - Goal: 500 subscribers before launch day

#### Week 2 — Waitlist & Community Building

- **Waitlist campaign:** "Join the waitlist, get 15% off your first order"
  - Landing page or Telegram bot `/start` → collect phone number
  - Share waitlist count publicly: "237 people are waiting — are you?"

- **Influencer seeding (micro-influencers, 5-10K followers)**
  - Send free product kits to 10-15 beauty/fashion micro-influencers in Addis
  - Ask them to post unboxing + honest review on launch day
  - Budget: ~5,000-10,000 ETB in product cost

- **User-generated content prompt:**
  - "Show us your current beauty routine — best video wins a free Sabacos kit"
  - Drives content + builds community

#### Week 3 — Product Showcase

- **Daily product spotlight (Instagram Stories + Telegram channel)**
  - One product per day: photo, price, key benefit, Amharic + English
  - "Which product should we feature tomorrow? Vote!"

- **Behind-the-scenes (TikTok)**
  - Warehouse/packaging process
  - "How your order gets to you in 24 hours"
  - Builds trust + transparency

- **Price anchor content:**
  - "This foundation costs 1,200 ETB in stores. On Sabacos? 899 ETB. Free delivery."
  - Comparison format works extremely well

#### Week 4 — Launch Countdown

- **7-day countdown series**
  - Day 7: "1 week to go! Here's what Sabacos looks like" (app preview video)
  - Day 6: "Free delivery on your first order — no minimum"
  - Day 5: "Meet our delivery team" (humanize operations)
  - Day 4: "We verified every product — here's how"
  - Day 3: "Your friend list is your discount" (referral teaser)
  - Day 2: "Tomorrow. 10 AM. Be ready."
  - Day 1: "TONIGHT. @SabacosShop. Don't miss it."

- **Influencer teaser posts** (paid or gifted)
  - 3-5 influencers post: "I just tried something amazing. Tomorrow you can too. 👀"

## 3. Launch Day

### Goal: Maximum visibility, first 100 orders, viral moment

#### Morning (10:00 AM)

- **Telegram channel blast:**
  - "🚀 SABACOS IS LIVE! Open the bot, shop now → [link]"
  - Include pinned message with bot link + instructions

- **Bot /start message update:**
  - Welcome message with inline "🛍️ Open Shop" button
  - First-time user gets 10% welcome discount auto-applied

- **Social media simultaneous posting:**
  - Instagram: Launch carousel (app screenshots, features, prices)
  - TikTok: "I just launched my own cosmetics shop on Telegram" (founder video)
  - Twitter: "We're live. 50+ products. Free delivery. Telegram-native. Let's go."
  - Facebook: Marketplace listing + group posts

#### Afternoon

- **Influencer posts go live** (coordinated for 12-3 PM)
  - Unboxing videos, honest reviews, swipe-up links

- **Instagram Live session:**
  - Founder + beauty influencer do a live "shop with me" session
  - Answer questions, show products, guide viewers through the app

- **Flash sale (2 hours only):**
  - "2-4 PM: Extra 20% off all lip products"
  - Creates urgency + drives immediate traffic

#### Evening

- **Thank you post:**
  - "Day 1 done! Thank you to everyone who ordered. Here's a peek at what's coming."
  - Share real customer screenshots (with permission)

- **User testimonial collection:**
  - DM first 50 customers: "How was your experience? Can we share your feedback?"

## 5. Paid Advertising Strategy

### Telegram Ads
- **Budget:** 5,000-10,000 ETB/month
- **Target:** Addis Ababa, ages 18-35, beauty/fashion interests
- **Format:** Sponsored messages in relevant channels
- **Creative:** "🛍️ Shop cosmetics on Telegram — Free delivery on first order → @SabacosShop"

### Instagram/Facebook Ads
- **Budget:** 3,000-7,000 ETB/month
- **Target:** Addis Ababa, 18-35, interests: beauty, skincare, fashion, online shopping
- **Format:** Reels ads, story ads, carousel ads
- **Creative A:** Before/after transformation
- **Creative B:** Price comparison (store vs. Sabacos)
- **Creative C:** Customer testimonial video
- **CTA:** "Shop Now" → Telegram bot deep link

### TikTok Ads
- **Budget:** 3,000-5,000 ETB/month
- **Format:** In-feed ads, spark ads (boost organic influencer content)
- **Creative:** Native-looking content, not polished ads
- **Best performers:** "I found the cheapest cosmetics in Addis" style

### Influencer Budget
- **Micro-influencers (5-15K followers):** 2,000-5,000 ETB per post OR product gifting
- **Mid-tier (50K-100K):** 10,000-20,000 ETB per post
- **Budget allocation:** 60% micro, 30% mid-tier, 10% macro
- **Monthly influencer spend:** 10,000-20,000 ETB

## 6. Referral Program Marketing

### "Invite & Earn" campaign

- **Mechanic:** Refer 3 friends → get 200 ETB store credit. Each friend gets 10% off first order.
- **Telegram-native:** Users share their referral link directly in chats
- **Social amplification:** "Share your link on your story, screenshot it, we'll feature you"
- **Leaderboard:** Top referrers each month win a free product bundle
- **Content angle:** "I made 1,500 ETB just by sharing with friends" (real user stories)

## 8. Competitive Advantages to Emphasize

1. **Telegram-native shopping** — no app download, no website login, just open the bot
2. **Verified authentic products** — every item guaranteed genuine
3. **Free delivery** — on orders above threshold (currently 2,000 ETB)
4. **Instant checkout** — pay with Telegram/Chapa in 2 taps
5. **Bilingual** — English + Amharic, full support
6. **Reward system** — every purchase earns points, spin the wheel for prizes
7. **Referral income** — earn real money by sharing with friends

## 9. Content Do's and Don'ts

### Do's
- ✅ Use Amharic in captions and voiceovers
- ✅ Show real people, real reactions
- ✅ Jump on trending sounds and formats within 24-48 hours
- ✅ Respond to every comment and DM within 1 hour
- ✅ Feature customer content (with permission)
- ✅ Use price anchoring ("Save X birr vs. store price")
- ✅ Create FOMO with limited stock alerts

### Don'ts
- ❌ Don't use stock photos — always original content
- ❌ Don't post in English only — Amharic drives engagement
- ❌ Don't ignore negative feedback — address it publicly and gracefully
- ❌ Don't oversell — 70% value content, 30% sales content
- ❌ Don't buy fake followers — Ethiopian audiences spot it instantly
- ❌ Don't post without a CTA — always tell people what to do next
